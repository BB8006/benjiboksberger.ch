Offer

For international charities, small NGOs and social startups.

If your team spends hours on admin, reporting or compliance, there is often a simple tool that could give that time back.

01 Free written check

Tell me how your team handles admin and reporting today. Within a week I send back one page: where the hours go and what a simple tool could fix.

02 Plan

If it makes sense, we plan it together: what the tool does, who uses it, what it costs and when it is ready. You get a short written proposal before anything is built.

03 Build

A simple internal tool for volunteers, HR, reporting or compliance. Built with the people who will use it, in their language, with regular check-ins.

04 Maintain

Hosting, fixes and small improvements, so the tool keeps working after the project ends.

Pricing

Small organisations pay little, larger ones pay more. I charge enough to get by, so the rest can go where it's needed. I'm still a student, so I take on one project at a time.
