About

Benji Boksberger, Business Informatics student and developer in Zürich.

Me

Travelling through Latin America, Africa and Asia, I met people living with the bare minimum. Not for lack of effort, but because the systems around them give them no support, security or infrastructure. I didn't want to be someone who only notices but someone who can help. Also, I didn't want to contribute with something that has nothing to do with what I'm learning or what I can already do.

So I wrote to NGOs all around and asked where coding could help. Child's Dream invited me to Chiang Mai.

Now I'm back in Zürich, finishing my degree at the University of Zurich. My bachelor's thesis is about unvoiced feedback across cultures: why people don't always say what they need, and how tools can still get it right.
